zh-freq.ts (the Mandarin reader's frequency prior) is a derived table,
built by scripts/gen-zh-freq.mjs. It holds, for characters with more than
one reading, their readings in order of use, and for some two thousand
common words a reading. It holds no definitions and no frequencies.

zh-words.ts (the common words, read as CC-CEDICT reads them) is a
derived table too, built by scripts/gen-zh-words.mjs. It holds some
three thousand CC-CEDICT words of two to six characters from wordfreq's
top 20,000, each with its reading (and, for some, their traditional
spellings). It holds no definitions and no frequencies.

from the first two; it picks its words with zh-freq.ts's readings):
