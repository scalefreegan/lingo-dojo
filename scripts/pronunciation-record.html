Before you start

  • Your browser will ask to use the microphone. A quiet room and a headset or laptop mic are fine.
  • Recordings stay on this computer. This page sends nothing anywhere else.
  • You can stop at any time and carry on later: every clip you keep is saved straight away.
  • Each clip is labelled with what you were aiming for, not what actually came out. A beginner's clips will contain real slips, and that is useful too. When you doubt one, tick "Not sure I said it right" before keeping it.
