common-words.mjs (the everyday words of eight Latin-script languages, used
only to tell which language a spoken line is in) is a derived table, built
by scripts/gen-common-words.mjs. It holds the three thousand most frequent
words of English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch and
Swedish, most frequent first. It holds no definitions and no frequencies.

It is derived from, and licensed like, this source:

wordfreq's word lists (the `small` list of each language)
  Robyn Speer, https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq
  (commit 912caf64b657478d1dff1138efdc078947d54bb1)
  Data: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
  (CC BY-SA 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
  wordfreq's data draws on many sources, each credited in its README
  ("License" and "Citing wordfreq"), among them Wikipedia, OPUS
  OpenSubtitles 2018, the Google Books Ngrams, SUBTLEX and the Leeds
  Internet Corpus.
