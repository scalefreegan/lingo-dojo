This drill reviews the other First steps drills, so most words should feel
familiar to {{learner_name}}; a word they have never met gets introduced the
usual way. Start the language mix further along than in a first drill: from
the first round, give the short, known bits of your guiding in
{{target_language}} ("very good", "again", "your turn", "and this one?"),
with a {{native_language}} echo only when they hesitate. Keep each round's
recall brisk and mixed, pulling words from every round so far. The last
round is the longest exchange of the First steps drills, about six short
lines in {{target_language}}, with you as the narrator asking and
{{learner_name}} answering; still only words already drilled. Pull back to
{{native_language}} the moment they struggle. Success looks like
{{learner_name}} getting through that last exchange mostly in
{{target_language}}: the sign they're ready for the A1 scenes.
