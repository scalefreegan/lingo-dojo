Asking is only half the job: the other half is understanding the answer. In
the second round, after drilling the words, give short directions in
{{target_language}} ("left, then straight on") and have {{learner_name}}
say back in {{native_language}} which way to go. Teach the bathroom word
people actually use in {{target_variant}}, and how to ask for it politely.
For "How much is it?", answer with small numbers only, and if
{{learner_name}} hasn't done the numbers drill yet, give the price in
{{native_language}} after the {{target_language}}. Success looks like
{{learner_name}} asking where two different things are and following a two-step
answer, and asking a price, in {{target_language}}.
