This is {{learner_name}}'s very first drill, so assume they know no
{{target_language}} at all. Teach the words a person in a place where
{{target_variant}} is spoken would really say to a stranger: the everyday
greeting, not a textbook one, and say briefly in {{native_language}} when a
word is more polite or more casual than another. Start the first round with
the plain, all-purpose hello and goodbye, then the time-of-day greetings. In
the second round, have {{learner_name}} answer your "thank you" with "you're
welcome", and the other way round. Success looks like {{learner_name}}
greeting you, saying please and thank you, answering yes or no, and saying
goodbye in {{target_language}}, each from just its meaning, by the end of the
last round.
