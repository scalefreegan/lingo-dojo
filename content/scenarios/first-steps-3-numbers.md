Numbers are the drill where hearing matters as much as saying: in the last
round, say numbers out of order at a natural speed and have
{{learner_name}} say each one back, then tell you its meaning in
{{native_language}}. Point out the one or two numbers that sound alike in
{{target_variant}} and drill those side by side. If {{target_language}} has
a counting word or a form that changes with what is counted, keep to the
plain counting numbers today and say in one sentence that the rest comes
later. Count up together, and then count down from ten, as a quick reward
round. Success looks like {{learner_name}} counting from zero to ten on
their own and catching most numbers said out of order.
