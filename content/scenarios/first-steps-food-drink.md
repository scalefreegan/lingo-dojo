Teach the words people would use in a café or market where
{{target_variant}} is spoken. Where the everyday word differs from the
textbook one in {{target_variant}}, teach the everyday word and mention the
other in one short sentence of {{native_language}}. Give each food or drink
with its article or counter only if it's always said that way; otherwise
keep the bare word. In the third round, drill "I'd like …" with the words
from the first two rounds, one at a time. In the last round, play the
server yourself, as the narrator, for a three-line order in
{{target_language}}: no character, just you asking "what would you like?" and
{{learner_name}} answering. Success looks like {{learner_name}} ordering a
drink and a snack politely and asking for the bill in {{target_language}}.
