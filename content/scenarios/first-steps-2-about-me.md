{{learner_name}} is still at the very start, so keep every phrase as short
as it naturally gets in {{target_variant}}. Use {{learner_name}}'s real name
and their real home country or city (ask them in {{native_language}} if you
don't know it, and say the country's name in {{target_language}} for them).
Where "Do you speak English?" applies, swap English for {{native_language}}.
Spend the most time on the third and fourth rounds: the lifesavers are the
phrases that keep a real conversation going later. From the third round on,
invite {{learner_name}} to use them with you for real, whenever they don't
understand or want something again. Success looks like {{learner_name}}
introducing themselves in two short lines and asking you to slow down or
repeat in {{target_language}} without being prompted.
