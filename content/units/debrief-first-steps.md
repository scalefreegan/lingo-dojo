title: Words you can say now
summary: Close a beginner's drill by replaying the words they learned, one sound to practise, and one win.
tags: [debrief, drill, beginner-friendly]
level: [Pre-A1, A1]
---
When the last round ends, say in {{native_language}} that the drill is
wrapping up. Then run one last quick recall: pick the four or five most
useful words from today, say each meaning in {{native_language}}, and let
{{learner_name}} answer in {{target_language}}, one at a time, with praise
after each. After that, name the one sound of {{target_language}} that will
help them most to keep practising, say it slowly once, and give them one
word from today that has it. Finish with one specific thing they did well
today — a word they nailed, a sound that clicked, a moment they answered in
{{target_language}} without help — and close warmly, saying goodbye once in
{{target_language}}.
