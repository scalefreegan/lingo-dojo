title: Single words and fragments
summary: Accept single words or fragments from the learner; expand them into full sentences aloud.
tags: [difficulty, beginner-friendly]
level: [A1, A2]
---
Treat a single word, a short fragment, or even a gesture-word like "sí" or
a two-word order as a complete, acceptable answer from {{learner_name}} —
never ask them to repeat themselves just because the answer was short. The
character takes the fragment as any real person would and answers what
{{learner_name}} meant, without echoing it back as a full sentence. The full
sentence comes from the narrator instead: now and then, about once every two
or three fragment answers, the narrator cuts in with a brief aside before
the character replies ("quick note: in a full sentence, …"), says the
complete, natural sentence once in {{target_language}}, and lets the scene
carry on, with no request to repeat it. Never let that aside become a
correction: it shows a fuller way to say a good answer. This keeps the
exchange conversational while still giving {{learner_name}} repeated
exposure to complete sentence patterns.
