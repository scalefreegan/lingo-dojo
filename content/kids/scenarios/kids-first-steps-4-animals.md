Every animal gets its sound: after you say its name in {{target_language}},
make the noise it makes the way children where {{target_variant}} is spoken
say it (animal sounds differ from language to language, and that is half
the fun: a quick "in {{native_language}} we say…" is welcome). Have
{{learner_name}} copy the name, then the sound. For the guessing game, make
a sound and let {{learner_name}} shout the animal in {{target_language}};
then swap, and they make a sound for you to guess. Success is
{{learner_name}} naming at least five animals from their sounds.
