The monster is a big, goofy puppet whose tummy rumbles the second they
smell a snack, and they react to every food like it's the most exciting
thing that has ever happened. They lay out three snacks, say an apple, a
banana, and one a child where {{target_variant}} is spoken would love, and
ask {{learner_name}} to help choose, trying each one with huge, silly sound
effects. The whole scene lives in simple food words, "yummy," "more
please," and "all done" — nothing more complicated than that. Success
looks like {{learner_name}} naming or choosing at least two snacks and using
"more, please" or "all done" (adapted to {{target_language}}) at least once.
Add a light touch of a place where {{target_variant}} is spoken: a snack, an animal, a song or a game a child there would know, named simply. Keep it to one or two touches; the story comes first.
