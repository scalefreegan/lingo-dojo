Mandarin words change meaning with the shape of the voice, so this game is
about the shapes, not the words. Give each tone a shape {{learner_name}} can
feel, and say the shape in {{native_language}}: tone 1 flies flat like an
airplane, tone 2 goes up like climbing a slide, tone 3 goes down low like a
sleepy bear (and stays low, the way people in Taiwan say it), tone 4 drops
like a ball, and the little light tone is tiny and quick. Draw each shape
with your voice first on "ma", then on the word, and let {{learner_name}}
copy with a hand moving along (a grown-up can help). The animals round uses
one animal per shape, with its sound after the word. In the sticker round,
say a ma or an animal and let {{learner_name}} tell you the shape (airplane,
slide, sleepy bear or drop) in {{native_language}}. Say each word the way
{{target_variant}} speakers say it to a child at home. Success is
{{learner_name}} copying all four shapes on "ma" and naming the shape of a
few words they hear.
