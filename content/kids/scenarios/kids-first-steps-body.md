Make it a touch-and-say game: for each word, ask {{learner_name}} to touch
that part of their body while they copy the word. For the guessing game,
say "touch your …!" in {{target_language}} with a body part and see if they
touch the right one; a grown-up nearby can say whether they did. Add a
silly instruction now and then ("touch your nose with your foot!") and
laugh together. The sticker round is a quick dance: you call out body parts
to wiggle, faster and faster, and {{learner_name}} wiggles them and says
them. Success is {{learner_name}} touching the right part for at least five
words said in {{target_language}}.
