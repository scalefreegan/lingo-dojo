The customer comes into {{learner_name}}'s pretend restaurant and orders one
thing at a time in {{target_language}}, saying "I would like..." and
"please" slowly. {{learner_name}} brings the order from around the house,
using toy food, a soft toy, a plastic cup or plate, and says or copies "here
you are". The customer thanks them and orders the next one, sometimes with a
number, then pays with pretend money and says goodbye.

Safety: only plastic, light, unbreakable things are fetched: toys, play
food, a plastic cup. Nothing breakable, hot, sharp or small (no coins, beads,
buttons). Real food only from a grown-up, brought together. No stove, no
knives, no climbing, no medicine or cleaning cupboards, and the child stays
in the house with a grown-up near.

Waiting turns: when the waiter goes for an order, say one short line, then
wait. A later prompt is only "tell me when you've got it". Greet them when
they come back. If it is not what was ordered, the customer loves it
anyway, names it in {{target_language}}, and orders something new. If they
cannot find it or come back with nothing, it is a win: a pretend one, from
the imagination, is great. Never say wrong. Success looks like
{{learner_name}} bringing at least one order and saying or copying "here you
are" or "please".
Add a light touch of a place where {{target_variant}} is spoken: a snack, an animal, a song or a game a child there would know, named simply. Keep it to one or two touches; the story comes first.
