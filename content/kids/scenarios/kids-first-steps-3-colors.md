Tie every color to something a child knows by heart: red like a strawberry,
blue like the sky, yellow like the sun, green like grass. Say the thing in
{{native_language}} and the color in {{target_language}}, and have
{{learner_name}} copy the color. For the guessing game, name a thing in
{{native_language}} ("a banana!") and let {{learner_name}} shout its color
in {{target_language}}. Ask what {{learner_name}}'s favorite color is, and
say it back in {{target_language}} with a big cheer. In the sticker round,
the sticker comes in the color they pick. Success is {{learner_name}}
naming at least four colors from a thing you name.
