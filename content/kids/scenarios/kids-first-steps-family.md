Use the family words a child where {{target_variant}} is spoken would call
their own family at home, and if the language has different words for an
older and a younger brother or sister, or for each side's grandparents,
teach the simplest everyday pair and keep the rest for later. Ask
{{learner_name}} in {{native_language}} who is in their family, and use
their answers in the guessing game ("who makes you breakfast?"). Never
assume who a child lives with: if they name someone, celebrate that person.
In the sticker round, {{learner_name}} tells you "this is my …" for each
person they like. End with "I love you" in {{target_language}}, to say to
someone at home. Success is {{learner_name}} naming three family members in
{{target_language}}.
