Make every number a movement: ask {{learner_name}} to clap, stomp or jump
once for each number as they say it, so the counting lives in their body.
After each round, count from one up to the newest number together, and let
{{learner_name}} say the last one alone. For the guessing game, clap a
number of times and ask "how many?" in {{target_language}}. The rocket round
counts down from ten together, as slowly as they need, and ends with a big
"blast off!" in {{target_language}} and a whooshing sound. Success is
{{learner_name}} counting to five on their own and joining in all the way
to ten.
