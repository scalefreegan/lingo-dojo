This is {{learner_name}}'s very first game in {{target_language}}, so they
know none of these words yet. Use the words a child where
{{target_variant}} is spoken would hear from family every day. Make hello
big and bright and goodbye slow and waving; whisper good night like it's
bedtime. For yes and no, ask silly questions in {{native_language}} ("do
cats say moo?") and let {{learner_name}} shout the {{target_language}} yes
or no. In the sticker round, you say hello and they answer, you give them a
pretend present and they say thank you, and you wave goodbye. Success is
{{learner_name}} copying every word at least once and saying hello and
goodbye on their own.
